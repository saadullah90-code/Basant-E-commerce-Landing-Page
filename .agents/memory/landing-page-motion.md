---
name: Landing-page motion
description: The user's motion and performance expectations for the URR Lahore landing page.
---

Keep animations smooth, lightweight, and tied to scrolling where appropriate. Avoid abrupt, janky motion or animations that make the page feel slow.

**Why:** The user repeatedly reported glitches and lag from the site's animations and asked for smoother, faster scroll-triggered motion.

**How to apply:** When adding or revising landing-page motion, prefer small opacity/transform reveals, limit always-running effects, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
