---
name: Public contact wording
description: Rules for naming and linking to the landing page's contact channel.
---

Use generic contact wording in all public page copy, accessibility labels, notices, and metadata. Keep contact actions labeled simply, such as “Contact us,” and preserve the configured contact destination.

**Why:** The user asked for the service name to appear nowhere on the website.

**How to apply:** Audit rendered text and accessible descriptions site-wide whenever contact copy changes; keep the external destination working without naming it in the interface.
